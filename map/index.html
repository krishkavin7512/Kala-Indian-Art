About this project

The geography of Indian art, made explorable.

Purpose
To explore the geographic spread and influence of different art styles across India, with images, descriptions and historical context about local art movements, artists and artworks at each location.
What's inside
35 locations across 16 states and union territories · 5 art forms from rock painting to modern movements · filters by art form and age · 4 animated trails of influence · a full-resolution photograph for every location · links into the Kāla timeline.
The map
Drawn from official Survey of India boundaries (via DataMeet) and projected with a Lambert conformal conic projection, as used for maps of India. Built in HTML, SVG and JavaScript with D3.
Sources
Photographs from Wikimedia Commons under open licences, credited on each place and on the credits page.
